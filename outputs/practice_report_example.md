# Prescribing review: Practice 03836 (A03836)

**North East and Yorkshire · ICB03 · list size 8,431 · IMD decile 8 · 17.8% aged 65+**

## Why this practice is on the list

After adjusting for the age and sex of your registered population, this practice issued **297 items for every 100 expected** across the six BNF sections in scope. The median practice in England sits at 97.

That is **outside the 99.8% control limit** on a funnel plot of all 6,300 English practices — meaning the gap is larger than random variation and ordinary between-practice differences together would produce at your list size.

**This is not a crude ranking.** Age and sex are already accounted for. Practices with older populations are expected to prescribe more, and that expectation is built into the figure above.

## What we are NOT saying

- We are not saying this prescribing is clinically wrong. We cannot see your patients.
- We are not saying it is unjustified. Age and sex are adjusted for; a specialist clinic, a nursing home on your list, or a local service you provide on behalf of others are not, and any of those would explain the gap entirely.
- We are not asking you to reduce prescribing to a target.

## What we are asking

One conversation, to establish whether there is a reason we cannot see in the data. If there is, we record it and this practice comes off the list — permanently, not until the next report.
